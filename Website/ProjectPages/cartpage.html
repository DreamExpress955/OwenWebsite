Production Base Transport Cart System

A production material-handling solution designed to allow stationary base dunnage to be moved without relying on a forklift, eliminating a recurring offload-station bottleneck.

AutoCAD Mechanical Design Manufacturing Material Handling Cost Estimation
Main Completed Cart Photo

THE PROBLEM

Production Offload Bottleneck

Production bases were placed onto dedicated dunnage after leaving the line. The existing dunnage did not have wheels, meaning a forklift was required every time a completed base needed to be moved away from the offload station.

When forklift movement was delayed, completed bases could accumulate at the offload station. Once the station became blocked, the production line could no longer offload additional bases.

These bottlenecks could cause approximately 15 to 30 minutes of production-line downtime.

DESIGN REQUIREMENTS

Designing Around the Existing Process

Rather than replacing the existing production dunnage, the cart system was designed around it. This allowed the existing dunnage to sit inside a mobile cart and gain the mobility required to clear the offload station.

Existing Dunnage

The cart needed to accommodate the production dunnage already being used for the different base configurations.

32 Base Variants

The completed cart system needed to support all 32 production base variants handled by the process.

Production Mobility

Wheels allowed loaded dunnage to be moved away from the offload area without waiting for immediate forklift availability.

Practical Manufacturing

The design needed to be practical to fabricate, assemble, procure, and deploy within an active manufacturing environment.

DESIGN PROCESS

From Production Floor to AutoCAD

I began by going directly onto the production line to study the existing dunnage and understand how the individual base configurations were being handled.

I created models of the existing dunnage and designed the cart system around those dimensions in AutoCAD. Three different cart lengths were required to cover the various base and dunnage configurations.

FINAL DESIGN

Three Cart Sizes, Nine Production Carts

The final solution used three different cart lengths to accommodate the range of production dunnage. Three carts of each length were produced, resulting in nine carts for production use.

RESULT

Eliminating the Bottleneck

Before implementation, congestion caused by these bases could delay the entire production line for approximately 15 to 30 minutes.

After the cart system was introduced, the loaded dunnage could be moved clear of the offload station without waiting for immediate forklift movement. The base-related congestion that had previously blocked the station was eliminated.
